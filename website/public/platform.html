WorkUnit Labs

Outsourced operations. Not another AI tool.

Companies need the work done. They do not need to hire engineers, keep models, or supervise a stack of agents. WorkUnit Labs takes a defined process, runs it on shared infrastructure, and returns completed work. You pay for what passes, not for seats, tokens, or hours.

01

Input

The documents, records, or events a unit receives. For invoices, that is a PDF, scan, or photo.

02

Execution

AI reads the work. Deterministic rules check it. The model does not get the last word.

03

Validation

A unit finishes only when the checks are true. Totals, duplicates, required fields, dates.

04

Exception

Anything that cannot be verified is flagged and reviewed. It is never guessed and never billed.

05

Output

A finished deliverable. For invoices, a file ready for QuickBooks Online or Xero.

06

Measurement

Accuracy, turnaround, and cost are counted on accepted work. That is the record.

Add-ons

Three add-ons. Only when the documents are there.

These do not run on a bare invoice. They run when you send the purchase order and receipt, the payment, or the close file. A gap in those documents is flagged. It is not invented.

Add-on

AP Match

Match the invoice to the purchase order and the receiving record. Quantity and price gaps are flagged before anyone pays.

Add-on

Cash Match

Match payments to open invoices, read the remittance, and separate a short pay from cash that does not apply.

Add-on

CloseReady

Prepare the month-end file. Missing support, breaks, and exceptions are listed before the close, not after it.

The units

One platform. A unit for each process.

Invoices are the work you can start today. AP Match, Cash Match, and CloseReady are add-ons. Everything else on this map is not offered yet.
