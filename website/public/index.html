FAQ

Questions firms ask us

Is this just OCR?

No. OCR turns an image into text. We return structured entries (vendor, dates, totals, tax and every line item) and check that the numbers reconcile before calling an invoice done.

What counts as an "accepted" invoice?

One that passed every check, or that failed a check and was then fixed and confirmed by our reviewers. Invoices we can't confirm come back flagged with the reason and aren't billed.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

Our checks catch most errors before they reach you: totals that don't reconcile, missing fields, duplicates and implausible dates. Those invoices go to a reviewer. If an accepted invoice turns out to be wrong, tell us and we'll credit it.

How fast is turnaround?

Our target is within one business day. Large backlogs are delivered in stages: most invoices within 24–48 hours, reviewed exceptions after.

What file types can we send?

PDFs, scans and phone photos saved as JPG or PNG. Multi-page invoices are fine; anything that isn't an invoice, like a quote or statement, is flagged rather than entered.

Do my clients have to change anything?

No. Keep collecting invoices the way you do now and forward them to the intake address, or drop them in a shared folder.

Which accounting software do you support?

QuickBooks Online and Xero files today. Tell us what you use in the pilot form and we'll tell you how we'd deliver.

We have tens of thousands of invoices. Can you handle that?

Yes. We start with a paid test of about 1,000 of your invoices so we can measure your documents, then quote a per-invoice price and a delivery schedule for the full job.

How much does each invoice cost?

Pilot is $99 one time for up to 200 invoices. Practice is $199/mo with 150 accepted invoices included ($1.33 each) and $1.25 per invoice after that. Firm is $499/mo with 400 included ($1.25 each) and $1.10 per invoice after that. Above Firm-plan volume, volume pricing runs from $1.40 down to $0.95–$1.00 per invoice (15,000+ a month on an annual contract); see the cost per invoice by monthly volume table. Invoices that fail a check are never billed.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

No setup fee and no long-term contract. The pilot is a one-time $99; monthly plans renew monthly and you can cancel any time.

Do you post bills or make payments?

No. We prepare clean entries; your team stays in control of posting and payments.
