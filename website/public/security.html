Security

Last updated November 2, 2026

Invoices are financial data, and Dovewood Capital, LLC, a Texas limited liability company doing business as WorkUnit Labs (“WorkUnit Labs,” “we,” “us”) handles them that way.

Our practices

  • Encryption in transit. Uploads, deliveries and this website use HTTPS.
  • Used only for your work. Documents are processed only to produce your entries and are never used to train AI models.
  • Limited access. Only the people working on your account can see your documents.
  • Retention you set. We agree a deletion schedule with you before the first batch, and delete data on request.
  • You stay in control. We never post entries or approve payments.

Subprocessors

  • Cloudflare – website hosting, content delivery and storage of form submissions.
  • An AI model provider – extraction of invoice fields, under terms that do not permit training on your data.
  • Stripe (if used) – payment processing. We do not store full card numbers.

We will tell clients before adding a new subprocessor that handles client documents.

Incidents

If we learn of a security incident affecting your data, we will notify affected clients without undue delay, explain what happened and what we're doing about it.
