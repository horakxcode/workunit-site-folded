Invoice field requirements

Every invoice is read and then checked against the rules below. An invoice is accepted when every required field is present and every check passes, or when a failed check is fixed and confirmed. Anything else is flagged. You pay only for invoices we deliver as accepted.

Required fields

If one is missing or unreadable, the invoice is flagged REQUIRED_MISSING with the field's name and sent to review.

  • Vendor name
  • Invoice number
  • Invoice date (a valid calendar date)
  • Currency
  • Subtotal
  • Tax, including a stated zero
  • Total
  • At least one line item with an amount

Optional fields

We capture these when they're on the invoice and show you when they're absent, but they never block acceptance.

  • Due date or payment terms
  • Vendor address
  • Vendor tax ID
  • PO number
  • Bill-to
  • Line quantity and unit price
  • Item codes
  • GL / account coding
  • Shipping
  • Discounts
  • Notes

Other checks on every invoice

  • Math: line amounts, subtotal, tax and total must add up to the cent. If quantity and unit price are both present, quantity times unit price must equal the line amount.
  • Currency: amounts are read in the invoice's currency. A mismatch is flagged only after a vendor profile has been saved. There is no assumption that every vendor bills in USD.
  • Tax rate: a stated tax, including zero, is accepted. Once a vendor profile has a rate, later invoices from that vendor are checked against it, within the tolerance on the profile (0.05 percentage points unless changed). We do not apply an 8.25% Texas default to vendors that have no profile.
  • Duplicates: checked against your history, both for the same vendor and invoice number and for the same vendor and total within 7 days.
  • Dates: invoice dates must be real calendar dates. Stale or future-dated invoices are flagged. A missing due date is not a failure.
  • Add-ons: AP Match, Cash Match, and CloseReady run only when you send the purchase order and receipt, the payment, or the close file. A missing add-on document is not a failed invoice.

Your WorkUnit Labs reviewer can change which fields are required for your account. Signed-in clients can see the current list under Client login → Invoice requirements.
